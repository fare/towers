# Towers of Implementations in Cubical Category Theory

This library describes Towers of Implementations, as first formalized
in [Francois-Rene Rideau's PhD thesis][1], on top of
the [cubical][2] and [cubical-categorical-logic][3] libraries.

[1]: https://fare.tunes.org/tmp/phd/thesis.pdf
[2]: https://github.com/agda/cubical
[3]: https://github.com/um-catlab/cubical-categorical-logic

[When observability composes](composition.md) documents the Agda records, the two composition lemmas, and a finite counterexample.

## Papers and Talks by Francois-Rene Rideau on the topic

### Papers

[Reconciling Semantics and Reflection — Formalisms, Protocols, Applications and Architecture](http://fare.tunes.org/tmp/phd/thesis.pdf),
unfinished PhD thesis (started 1998, largely rewritten circa 2016).

[“Climbing Up the Semantic Tower — at Runtime”](https://github.com/fare/climbing),

[Off the Beaten Track (OBT)](https://conf.researchr.org/track/POPL-2018/OBT-2018)

[Reflection with First-Class Implementations](https://github.com/fare/fci2017),
a paper ([PDF](http://fare.tunes.org/files/fci2017/fci.pdf),
[HTML](http://fare.tunes.org/files/fci2017/fci.html))
I submitted at [SNAPL 2017](http://snapl.org/2017/cfp.html),
but was rejected for being too abstract without implementation or examples.
